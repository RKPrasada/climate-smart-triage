# Paediatric Triage Protocols
## Eastern Ghats Implementation — Paderu and Araku Valley

All protocols are derived from WHO Integrated Management of Childhood Illness
(IMCI) guidelines 2023, adapted for the disease burden and climate context of
the Eastern Ghats tribal regions.

---

## Age-Adjusted Respiratory Rate Thresholds (WHO IMCI)

| Age group | Normal range | Danger threshold |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Newborn 0–2 months | 30–60 | **above 60** |
| Infant 2–12 months | 25–50 | **above 50** |
| Child 1–5 years | 20–40 | **above 40** |

Count for a full 60 seconds. Repeat if the child is crying.

---

## Universal Danger Signs — Immediate Referral

Any of the following in a child under 5 requires immediate referral
regardless of other findings:

- Unable to drink or breastfeed
- Vomits everything
- Has had convulsions during this illness
- Lethargic or unconscious
- Severe respiratory distress (grunting, nasal flaring, severe chest indrawing)

---

## Climate-Adjusted Triage Logic

### Monsoon Season (June–September)

Any fever in a child under 5 during the monsoon should raise suspicion for:
1. Malaria — especially if cyclical, with rigors, or if the child is lethargic
2. Diarrhoeal disease — especially within 2 weeks of local flooding

Perform a malaria rapid diagnostic test (RDT) if available.
Treat any suspected severe malaria as URGENT.

### Heat Season (April–May)

During a heat wave (heat index above 40 C), any of the following in a child
under 1 year is a warning:
- Temperature above 38.5 C
- Poor feeding or lethargy
- Dry mouth or reduced urine output

Move the child to a cool place. Tepid sponging. Offer fluids.
Refer if temperature does not fall within 30 minutes or lethargy persists.

**Neonates (0–28 days):** any fever during a heat wave = IMMEDIATE referral.

### Post-Monsoon (October–November)

Peak malaria mortality period. Any fever in a child = malaria until proven otherwise.
Perform RDT. If positive: URGENT referral.
If negative but fever persists beyond 2 days: URGENT referral.
