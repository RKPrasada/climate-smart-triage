## **ClimateSmartTriage: Resilient Healthcare for the Last Mile**

In the tribal corridors of Paderu and Araku Valley, climate change isn’t an abstract threat—it is a barrier to survival. When monsoons wash out roads and heatwaves collapse mobile networks, community health workers are often left isolated. **ClimateSmartTriage** is an Android-based solution designed for these "offline" realities, ensuring that life-saving clinical logic remains functional when the world goes dark.

### **Intelligence Without Connectivity**

At the heart of the app is **Google Gemma 2B**, a lightweight AI model fine-tuned on WHO pediatric guidelines and regional climate-health data. Optimized for $150 USD smartphones, the model runs entirely on-device.

* **Climate-Aware Logic:** By caching local forecasts, the AI automatically adjusts its suspicion index. If it is "Monsoon Week Five," it prioritizes malaria and waterborne pathogens.
* **Proactive Triage:** During peak heatwaves, the app flags newborns for heat-stress monitoring *before* clinical symptoms escalate.
* **Instant Action:** Within five seconds, workers receive triage instructions in plain Telugu or English.

### **Sovereignty and Privacy by Design**

We replace traditional "data collection" with **community data sovereignty**.

* **Identity:** Households hold cryptographic identities via **Hyperledger Indy**, managed by local tribal agencies.
* **Transparency:** Every data access request is logged on an immutable **Hyperledger Besu** audit trail.
* **Federated Learning:** The AI improves through decentralized updates. Devices contribute "knowledge" (model weights) rather than raw patient records, ensuring sensitive data never leaves the village.

### **Open-Source Scalability**

Built for the **UNICEF Venture Fund (2026)**, ClimateSmartTriage is fully **Apache 2.0 licensed**. By combining edge computing with blockchain-backed consent, we provide a blueprint for state health ministries to protect vulnerable populations against the compounding crises of climate and disease.
